## 🔍 Engineering Perspective

I approach engineering from the perspective of **systems rather than isolated scripts**.

When building a data product, I care about:

- **Reliability** — retries, timeouts, validation and failure handling
- **Data quality** — normalization, deduplication, enrichment and traceability
- **Architecture** — clear boundaries between ingestion, storage, services and products
- **Maintainability** — readable code, testing, typing and documentation
- **Security** — defensive validation and security-oriented review
- **Observability** — logs, health tracking and operational visibility
- **Business relevance** — building systems around decisions, not scraping for its own sake

> **Don't scrape without a business question.**
